Insurance Renewal Summary

Townhomes at Charles Park Condominium Trust · West Roxbury, MA 02132 · 18 Residential Units · 09/30/2026 – 09/30/2027
Version 2September 24, 2026
Executive Summary

The association is insured with Travelers through Guardian Insurance on a property and liability package with a separate umbrella. Travelers’ renewal is $33,738, up $1,935 or 6.1%. The same agency quoted The Hartford at $24,953, $8,785 below the Travelers renewal; a second agency took the account to market and its indications came back higher. Both options insure the buildings at $15,499,426 on a $5,000 deductible. The Hartford option adds employment practices liability and a limited cyber cover, and insures equipment breakdown to the full building limit. It carries a $4,000,000 umbrella against $5,000,000 and a $500,000 per-claim limit on trustee protection (D&O) against $1,000,000. Its trustee protection starts September 30, 2026; Travelers covers prior acts without limit. On price the data points toward The Hartford; on liability limits it points toward Travelers. The by-laws set a fidelity bond floor of $156,628: The Hartford option carries $200,000 that does not extend to the management company, and Travelers carries $25,000 with a $200,000 quote pending.

Coverages Not Carried & What They Would Cost
Fidelity Bond · $200,000 Covering the Management Company · Estimated $400 – $900 a yearThe by-laws require a bond of not less than one and one-half times annual operating expenses and reserves — $156,628 on the 2026 budget — covering trustees, volunteers and the management company. The Hartford option carries $200,000 but does not extend to the property manager; Travelers carries $25,000 with a $200,000 limit requested. It pays when association funds are taken by a trustee, a volunteer or management company staff — the property policy does not respond to theft of money. Both carriers have been asked to name the management company.

About RenewFlo
www.renewflo.co

RenewFlo is a neutral coordination service for condominium associations. We run the insurance renewal from end to end — organizing the underwriting data, engaging brokers and carriers, and turning their quotes into one clear, apples-to-apples comparison for the board.

Most boards only ever see what a single broker chooses to show them, in a format built to sell rather than to compare. Deductible changes get buried, missing coverages go unmentioned, and competing quotes never quite line up — yet the renewal is one of the largest checks an association writes each year.

Our model keeps us neutral by design: RenewFlo is compensated by the broker whose program the board ultimately selects — never by the association. We are paid the same no matter which option wins, so our only job is making sure the data in front of the board is complete, accurate, and easy to act on.
