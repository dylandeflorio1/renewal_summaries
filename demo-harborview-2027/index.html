Executive Summary

The association is insured with Example Mutual on a property and liability package, with D&O and crime from Demo Casualty, a $10,000,000 umbrella from Sample Specialty and workers compensation from Placeholder Workers Comp. The renewal program is $70,680, up $3,960 or 6%. Example Mutual's package renews at $56,720, up $3,210 or 6%. The building limit rises $640,000 to $22,040,000. At $25,000 deductibles the package is $53,980, $2,740 below the renewal. Harbor Point Mutual priced the package at $51,940, $4,780 below the renewal, with a higher sewer backup limit, a lower ordinance or law limit and no hired and non-owned auto. On price the data points toward Harbor Point; on ordinance or law and auto liability it points toward the renewal.

About RenewFlo
www.renewflo.co

RenewFlo is a neutral coordination service for condominium associations. We manage the insurance renewal from start to finish: we organize the underwriting data, coordinate with brokers and carriers, and turn the resulting quotes into one clear, side-by-side comparison for the board.

Insurance renewals are complex, and most boards are volunteers working with limited time. Quotes arrive from different carriers in different formats, with different deductible structures, sublimits, and exclusions, so a true comparison is hard even when everyone involved is doing good work. Changes from the expiring program can be easy to miss, and the renewal is one of the largest checks an association writes each year. RenewFlo works alongside brokers to present each program clearly, so the board can make a confident decision.

Our model keeps us neutral by design. RenewFlo is compensated by the broker whose program the board selects, never by the association. We are paid the same no matter which option wins, so our only job is helping the board see every option clearly and act on it.
