Executive Summary

The association is insured with Travelers on a property and liability package, with a separate PMA crime policy. Travelers did not offer renewal terms following the $1,600,000 sprinkler-failure claim of February 2026. RenewFlo placed the account with a specialty brokerage whose market is accounts carrying open large losses, and two programs came back. Option 1 insures the full building value on one property policy for $91,723, up $57,649, with a $25,000 deductible. The same policy at the $10,000 deductible the Trust requires is Option 1B at $130,598 — $38,875 more for $15,000 of deductible. Option 2 splits the building value across a primary and an excess property policy for $104,459 with a $100,000 deductible. All three add trustee protection (D&O), not carried before, and raise the fidelity bond to $350,000, the level the Trust requires; an umbrella and earthquake coverage are available as add-ons. Surplus-lines pricing follows the loss history: as the open claims close and loss-free years accumulate, the account becomes eligible for standard-market carriers again and the premium moves down with it.

The Trust's $10,000 deductible cap is out of step with the market
Section 5.8.3 of the Declaration of Trust caps the property deductible at $10,000. Curotech will honor it only as Option 1B — $38,875 more premium each year for $15,000 less deductible, 2.6 times the protection it buys. Option 2 cannot meet the cap. The underwriter's position is that with the open $1,600,000 claim and the 2024 water loss, a lower deductible adds exposure to small recurring losses. The data points toward amending Section 5.8.3 (75% of the beneficial interest) and binding Option 1 at $25,000; the same section already lets the trustees assess a deductible to the unit where the loss occurred.
How the per-unit water deductible works
Option 1 applies $25,000 to each unit damaged (floor $50,000 per event); Option 1B applies $10,000 per unit. A leak reaching four units carries $100,000 under Option 1 — the figure Option 2 applies to any loss — and $40,000 under Option 1B. Deductibles assessed to owners are recoverable under their HO-6 loss-assessment coverage.

About RenewFlo
www.renewflo.co

RenewFlo is a neutral coordination service for condominium associations. We run the insurance renewal from end to end — organizing the underwriting data, engaging brokers and carriers, and turning their quotes into one clear, apples-to-apples comparison for the board.

Most boards only ever see what a single broker chooses to show them, in a format built to sell rather than to compare. Deductible changes get buried, missing coverages go unmentioned, and competing quotes never quite line up — yet the renewal is one of the largest checks an association writes each year.

Our model keeps us neutral by design: RenewFlo is compensated by the broker whose program the board ultimately selects — never by the association. We are paid the same no matter which option wins, so our only job is making sure the data in front of the board is complete, accurate, and easy to act on.
